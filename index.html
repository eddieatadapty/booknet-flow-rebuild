Your ads × your app

Three Meta promises, all sent to the web. The app could keep each one.

Your Meta ads sell a story: “Vuelve a leer GRATIS”, “Libro que te hará odiar al protagonista… y después te hará enamorarte de él”, a Ukrainian video for “Халепа для олігарха”. Every one of them links to the book page on booknet.com or booknet.ua, in the browser. Your iOS app, Booknet – electronic books, is where those pages point next.

Flow Builder can open a different first launch by the campaign an install came from, so the app opens on the book from the ad, the free chapters come before any account, and each ad is judged on the books its readers buy.
